## Notes on use

- **Never send it without the "why this company" line filled in.** One specific sentence beats three paragraphs of enthusiasm.
- **Address a person where you can.** Find the engineering manager or a recruiter on LinkedIn. "Hi [Name]" outperforms "Dear Hiring Team".
- **Attach the CV as a PDF** named `Khaled_Alsanafi_Resume.pdf`.
- **Say the numbers once.** Repeating metrics from the CV in the letter is fine; expanding them is not.
- **Visa line:** include it for roles abroad, leave it out for Malaysia-based roles. Stating it plainly filters out employers who can't sponsor, which saves weeks.
